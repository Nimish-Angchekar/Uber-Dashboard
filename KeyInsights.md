# Key Insights

- **148.8K bookings** generated a total booking value of **₹5.18 Cr**, with a **62.2% completion rate**.
- **Auto** recorded the highest booking volume at **37.4K rides**, followed by Go Mini and Go Sedan.
- **UPI** was the most-used payment method with **45.8K bookings**, followed by Cash with 25.3K.
- **25.2% of bookings were cancelled**, with driver cancellations accounting for the larger share.
- **₹5.18 Cr** in booking value was generated from **92.6K completed rides**, with an average booking value of approximately **₹560**.
- **Viswhavidyalaya** was the top pickup location by booking volume and booking value.
- Average customer and driver ratings were **4.40 and 4.23**, indicating generally positive trip experiences.
- Average **VTAT was 8.46 minutes**, while average **CTAT was 29.15 minutes**.
- Vehicle-wise cancellation rates were relatively consistent, remaining close to **25%** across categories.
- Overall, the dashboard highlights **strong booking demand, Auto as the highest-volume vehicle type, UPI as the dominant payment method, and cancellations as a key operational area**.
